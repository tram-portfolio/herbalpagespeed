Welcome to Herbal Spa - Top spa in Da Nang

Located in the heart of Da Nang City, Herbal Spa is a trusted spa and wellness center offering relaxing and restorative experiences with over 10 years of expertise. With 3 branches across Da Nang and Hoi An, Herbal Spa has become one of Central Vietnam’s most recognized wellness destinations.

Herbal Spa provides a peaceful 5-star atmosphere with skilled therapists, professional treatments, and natural herbal products designed to help guests relax, recharge, and restore overall well-being. Welcoming thousands of visitors from more than 30 countries each month, the spa is highly appreciated for its consistent service quality and deeply relaxing experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Herbal Spa Da Nang so popular with customers?
Herbal Spa Da Nang is loved by many customers for its premium, clean, and peaceful treatment space that offers a truly relaxing experience. In addition, the spa features highly skilled and experienced therapists, along with a wide range of services from relaxation treatments to specialized therapies, catering to diverse customer needs.

What special benefits and offers does Herbal Spa provide for guests?
After each session, guests at Herbal Spa are served a complimentary light refreshment such as hot soup, mango dessert, fruits, coconut cake, and herbal tea (the menu may vary by season). In addition, the spa regularly offers special promotions and traveler-exclusive deals; please check our website or contact us directly for the latest updates.

Does Herbal Spa offer private rooms for couples or families?
Yes. Herbal Spa provides private treatment rooms for couples and families, ensuring comfort and privacy. Please inform us in advance when making your reservation so we can arrange the most suitable space.

Can I take a shower before or after my treatment at the spa?
Absolutely. Herbal Spa offers clean shower facilities for guests to use before or after their treatment. Simply inform our reception staff for assistance.

Are personal lockers available at the spa?
Yes. Each guest is provided with a personal locker to securely store belongings throughout their visit to Herbal Spa.

Does Herbal Spa accept credit cards or international payments?
Yes. Herbal Spa accepts international credit cards such as Visa and MasterCard, as well as other international and contactless payment methods. Please contact us in advance if you plan to use a specific payment option.
